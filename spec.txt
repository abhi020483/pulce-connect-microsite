PULSE CONNECT is a three-part hybrid education series designed for cardiologists and heart failure specialists across Asia. Each chapter addresses a critical gap in heart failure management — from early GDMT implementation to managing complex patients and personalising cardiovascular care. The series features international and regional faculty, interactive panel discussions, and case-based learning.

Chapter Title
CHAPTER 1
Closing the GDMT Gap: From Evidence to Early Implementation
Event Details
📍 Manila, Philippines
📅 28 October 2026 | 7:00–9:00 PM PHT
🎥 Hybrid (In-person + Virtual)- Keep a placeholder for the images of the faculty for this event 
________________


Key Learning Objectives
1. Understand the current treatment gap in Heart Failure and its impact on patient outcomes
2. Review the latest evidence supporting early and comprehensive initiation of Guideline-Directed Medical Therapy (GDMT) in HFrEF patients
3. Recognize the clinical benefits of rapid GDMT implementation compared with traditional sequential treatment approaches
4. Identify barriers leading to clinical inertia and delayed optimization of heart failure therapy
5. Explore practical strategies to ensure seamless transition from hospital-based care to long-term outpatient management while maintaining GDMT adherence
6. Develop actionable approaches for translating guideline recommendations into routine clinical practice to improve patient survival and quality of life

Chapter Title
CHAPTER 2
Managing Challenging Heart Failure Patients
Event Details
📍 Bangkok, Thailand
📅 30 October 2026 | 3:00–5:00 PM ICT
🎥 Hybrid (In-person + Virtual)- Keep a placeholder for the images of the faculty for this event 


________________


Key Learning Objectives
1. Understand the challenges of implementing GDMT in complex heart failure patients with low blood pressure, chronic kidney disease, and other high-risk clinical conditions
2. Learn evidence-based approaches for optimizing heart failure therapies in patients with multiple comorbidities
3. Evaluate practical titration strategies to achieve target doses while maintaining safety and tolerability
4. Gain confidence in managing treatment-related concerns such as hypotension, renal dysfunction, electrolyte imbalance, and drug interactions
5. Discuss real-world barriers to GDMT implementation and explore solutions from regional and international expert experiences
6. Apply guideline recommendations effectively in challenging clinical scenarios to maximize patient outcomes

PAGE 7 — CHAPTER 3 DETAIL PAGE
Chapter Title
CHAPTER 3
Personalizing Cardiovascular Care Through Patient Profiling
Event Details
📍 Jakarta, Indonesia
📅 31 October 2026 | 3:30–5:30 PM WIB
🎥 Hybrid (In-person + Virtual)- Keep a placeholder for the images of the faculty for this event 


________________


Key Learning Objectives
1. Understand the role of patient profiling in enabling individualized cardiovascular and heart failure treatment decisions
2. Identify the most appropriate heart failure patient populations for ARNI therapy across different stages of the treatment journey
3. Learn how clinical characteristics, risk factors, and comorbidities influence therapeutic selection and outcomes
4. Develop a precision medicine approach for balancing thrombotic and bleeding risks in cardiovascular patients
5. Translate patient profiles into evidence-based therapeutic choices to achieve better clinical outcomes
6. Explore emerging concepts in personalized cardiovascular medicine and their application in everyday practice
