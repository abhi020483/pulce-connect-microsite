Program summary

PULCE Connect is a three-part hybrid education series designed for cardiologists and heart failure specialists across Asia. Each chapter addresses a critical gap in heart failure management — from early GDMT implementation to managing complex patients and personalising cardiovascular care. The series features international and regional faculty, interactive panel discussions, and case-based learning.

What you will learn

  1. Closing the GDMT Gap: From Evidence to Early Implementation
  2. Managing Challenging Heart Failure Patients
  3. Personalizing Cardiovascular Care Through Patient Profiling
