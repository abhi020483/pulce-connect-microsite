What is PULCE Connect?
PULCE Connect is a three module education series designed for cardiologists and heart failure specialists across.
Each chapter addresses a critical gap in heart failure management — from early GDMT implementation to managing complex patients and personalising cardiovascular care. The chapters features international and regional faculty & interactive panel discussions, followed by Q&A session


What will I learn?
Explore three key areas of contemporary heart failure management:
* IMPLEMENT: Closing the GDMT Gap – From Evidence to Early Implementation.
* OPTIMIZE: Managing Challenging Heart Failure Patients – Complexity, comorbidities and treatment optimisation.
* INDIVIDUALIZE: Personalising Cardiovascular Care – Through Patient Profiling.
3. Who will I learn from?
Learn from Prof. Gianluigi Savarese, Professor of Cardiology at Karolinska Institute and President-Elect of the Heart Failure Association of the ESC, alongside distinguished regional cardiology faculty.
Gain international scientific insights complemented by regional clinical perspectives and practical, case-based discussions.
4. How can I participate?
Attend In Person | Join Live Webcast | Access On Demand
Join the scientific sessions live from:
* Manila, Philippines: 28 October 2026
* Bangkok, Thailand: 29 October 2026
* Jakarta, Indonesia: 31 October 2026
One registration provides access to all three sessions, with the flexibility to participate live or continue learning on demand.
